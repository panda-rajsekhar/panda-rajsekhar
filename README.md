### 🧩 Philosophy

> "Build systems that are not just functional, but meaningful."

I like sitting at the intersection of **electronics and software** — taking an idea from a schematic or a register map all the way to a working, polished implementation. Whether it's bare-metal firmware, a full-stack app, or a security research rabbit hole, the goal is always the same: make it real, make it work, make it matter.
